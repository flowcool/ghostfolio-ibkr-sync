# corporate-action-detection Specification

## Purpose
Detect IBKR corporate actions and explain their effect before related imports. Keep unsafe BUY/SELL on hold while preserving independent work and all existing financial-write boundaries.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Source-backed corporate-action inventory

Each valid source-account report SHALL inventory every CorporateAction row before affected financial work. Associate rows by verified source account and event evidence. Equal symbols, amounts or dates alone SHALL NOT join events. Retain original row fields, source hashes, effective/report dates, signed quantities, contract IDs, old/new ISINs, ratios and cash fields. Distinguish missing fields from observed zero. Reject nonfinite numbers and conflicting account/event evidence. Row order and repeated identical rows SHALL NOT create extra events. Conflicting repeats SHALL remain visible and unresolved.

The initial observed shapes SHALL be a forward split whose description states a ratio and a reverse split with linked withdrawal/addition legs and an identity change. Parsing an unambiguous positive integer ratio from the description SHALL establish a candidate ratio only. For a forward split, independently prove the pre-event quantity from a complete source trade/event chain or an authoritative source position, then require exact added quantity = before quantity × (ratio − 1). A destination quantity inferred from that same ratio SHALL NOT provide independent proof. For a reverse split, reconcile both legs, their identities and the description ratio exactly. Identifying a source event SHALL NOT certify its financial treatment. Missing legs, unsupported types, revisions and cancellations SHALL remain visible without guessed transformations. A description alone SHALL NOT prove identity continuity, coverage, before/after quantities or cash. No general IBKR actionID stability guarantee is established.

CorporateActions SHALL be a required section in each verified source report. A selected empty section SHALL mean no action row observed in that report window. A missing section SHALL mean unavailable action coverage and hold all BUY/SELL for that source account, while independent cash and other accounts retain existing guards. A present section with incomplete required row fields SHALL hold the conservatively bounded scope. Source-field validation SHALL require accountId, type, actionID, transactionID, levelOfDetail, security/contract identity, quantity, currency, dateTime with timezone, reportDate and description/actionDescription for the supported shapes. Retain and classify missing cash fields separately. These observed fields do not guarantee event completeness. Setup guidance SHALL list the section and fields. OpenPositions SHALL NOT become a new mandatory query section.

Baseline: README conversion table and existing parsing tests select Trade and dividend records. The saved real reports inspected on 2026-10-24 add four CorporateAction rows for three events. Sanitized fixtures SHALL preserve their structure without private identifiers. Checks below are implementation acceptance commands; they are not current passing evidence.

#### Scenario: Observed shapes and incomplete evidence
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k inventory` replays sanitized forward/reverse shapes, permutations, duplicates, changed IDs, malformed fields, unknown types, missing legs, wrong accounts, absent versus empty sections and a description ratio without independent pre-event quantity
- **THEN** three source events are identified once in the baseline, without claiming three releasable financial treatments; every counterexample has a bounded unresolved reason; no activity or native split is created.

### Requirement: Separate quantity, identity, history and cash conclusions

Each event finding SHALL show independent conclusions for source completeness, security identity, effective quantity, stored historical cost/dates and event cash. Conclusions SHALL distinguish observed agreement, observed discrepancy and missing proof. No current quantity match SHALL certify acquisition cost, dates or old/new identity. No cost/date difference alone SHALL make a proven identity and effective quantity unsafe.

Compare source units with complete destination activities, relevant profile splits and fresh holdings for the mapped destination account. Identify original trades plus native adjustment, already-normalized activities, or unresolved reconstruction. Apply effective dated ratios exactly once for comparison. Raw BUY minus SELL totals SHALL NOT certify a native-adjusted position. Inventory the profile and its use across destination accounts visible in the complete activity listing. Release SHALL prove the target account's identity and effective quantity, including every profile-wide ratio that affects it. Do not require IBKR history for an unrelated manual or other-broker account solely because it shares the profile. Do not ignore shared-profile evidence that contradicts or prevents isolating the target's calculation; such uncertainty SHALL retain the target hold. No profile mutation is in scope.

For an ISIN transition, complete linked source legs SHALL establish old/new security association. Both nonempty ISINs SHALL resolve through explicit operator mappings to the same active destination data-source/symbol profile. Missing mappings, fallback ticker equality, a null profile ISIN, or equal quantities alone SHALL NOT prove continuity. An explicit conflicting destination ISIN SHALL block release. Mapping agreement SHALL be necessary, not sufficient: require unique source/destination ownership, no competing alias holding and exact quantity reconciliation. The connector SHALL NOT create or change mappings. A resolved, fully closed position SHALL require its complete trade/event reconciliation; a missing holding or numeric zero alone SHALL NOT prove resolution.

Keep processing dateTime, reportDate and independently established market effective date distinct. Neither broker processing time nor report publication date SHALL silently become the effective date. Use a proven effective date and the qualified destination's strict date comparison semantics for candidate classification. Unknown effective timing or an ambiguous boundary-day trade SHALL remain held. Lab fixture dates SHALL NOT certify a market effective-date rule.

CORPORATE_ACTION_EVIDENCE_FILE MAY name a private, operator-curated keyed YAML input. Version 1 SHALL contain `events`, keyed first by source account and then by actionID. Each record SHALL name `effective_date` (YYYY-MM-DD), `source_url` (an official HTTPS source), `document` (a local regular file relative to the evidence input) and `sha256` (that document's digest). The operator SHALL verify the date against that official document. The connector SHALL validate the binding, date, private paths and digest, retain immutable document bytes and reconstruct the input each run. It SHALL make no request to source_url. Invalid or missing proof SHALL retain the affected hold. This evidence supplies a dated fact, never an acknowledgment or write approval. It SHALL NOT prove source coverage, quantity or identity. Changes to evidence SHALL require fresh source/destination reconciliation; edited cached conclusions SHALL remain non-authoritative. The operator approved this source choice on 2026-10-25.

Cash SHALL be supported by explicit linked source records and complete declared windows. Zero action cash fields with no linked cash row SHALL be reported as no cash component observed in that evidence, never as a universal guarantee. Fractional entitlement, compensation, tax and currency conversion SHALL NOT be invented. Distinct ordinary dividends SHALL retain existing validation and SHALL NOT become split cash by date proximity.

#### Scenario: Independent conclusions

- **THEN** quantities, identity, cost/dates and cash receive separate reasons; adjustment is not doubled; missing identity and fractional cash remain unresolved; a cost-only discrepancy does not hold proven quantities.

### Requirement: Conservative import holds and evidence-based release

Before dispatch, unresolved corporate-action identity or effective quantity SHALL hold BUY/SELL for every provably affected security, including both old/new aliases. If that set cannot be bounded within the verified source account, hold all BUY/SELL for that account. Do not infer a global account set from an unverified row. Existing source-account validation SHALL stop mismatched reports first. Unsupported evidence SHALL use the same scope rules. Continue unrelated securities, later accounts and independent cash under their existing guards.

Before any review or import decision, establish all configured source owners of each destination. If multiple owners share a destination and any owner has a known corporate action or unavailable source evidence, every co-owner SHALL hold all BUY/SELL. Unavailable evidence includes a missing report, a failed parse or validation, and an unresolved destination binding. Use configured destination names and resolved IDs conservatively; an unknown owner SHALL NOT be treated as event-free. This hold SHALL be independent of account order. Sources with distinct proven destinations SHALL retain independent processing and cash guards.

Existing activity-review proposals for held BUY/SELL SHALL remain visible and non-actionable. No yes, deleted proposal, acknowledgment or edited state SHALL bypass a corporate-action hold. Unsupported action types SHALL remain held where their identity or quantity effect cannot be proved; adding a new treatment requires a separately approved spec revision. Reports SHALL state that limitation and the missing evidence, not promise automatic release. Known pending correction uncertainty SHALL retain its stronger whole-account financial fence. Corporate-action evidence SHALL not authorize a correction, mapping change or new import.

Release SHALL require complete source evidence and freshly revalidated destination identity and effective-quantity evidence for the held scope. Previously validated source bytes retained with their original acquisition bindings SHALL remain historical evidence after an event leaves the current report window. Reparse those bytes and compare their hashes; do not trust cached conclusions. Current acquisitions SHALL be checked for conflicting or revised event evidence. A retained historical report SHALL NOT be called a fresh broker confirmation or proof that an unobserved revision never happened. Missing required chain, dates, identities or coverage SHALL keep the finding unresolved. A report window that no longer includes the event, a disappeared activity, an absent profile or missing state SHALL NOT prove resolution. Rebuild selection and effective holdings after release. Normal deduplication, acceptance and sale guards SHALL still decide whether an import is safe. Simulated accepted buys SHALL NOT authorize a real sale. Known effective quantities SHALL update only from proven accepted activity results.

Release SHALL be candidate-specific. In an already-normalized history without a proven native adjustment, hold any unmatched pre-event BUY/SELL whose raw units would change the reconciled representation. Do not convert that candidate or import it merely because the current position agrees. Apply the same unit proof to proposed review replacements. Recompute affected corporate-action conclusions and effective holdings after every confirmed correction and before any next correction/import. A later evidence mismatch SHALL stop dependent BUY/SELL; stronger uncertain-write fences still win. Retain unrelated work under its existing guards.

#### Scenario: Bounded holds and release
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k holds` exercises two accounts, aliases, unknown scope, old events outside the next window with retained source bytes, unsupported types, manual acknowledgment, existing yes approvals, unmatched pre-event trades, effective-date ambiguity, post-correction drift, uncertain corrections and later complete evidence
- **THEN** only the justified BUY/SELL scope is held, independent cash continues unless a stronger existing fence applies, and only fresh destination proof with valid current or retained source evidence releases the hold before normal selection.

#### Scenario: Missing evidence in a shared destination
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k shared_owner_unavailable` covers two owners with a shared target, missing or malformed reports, failed account validation, unresolved targets, both source orders and distinct-target controls
- **THEN** unavailable co-owner evidence holds every shared owner's BUY/SELL before any correction or import; distinct destinations and independent cash retain their existing guards.

### Requirement: Native evidence requires a qualified destination

Accept native split evidence only after an authenticated read establishes a stable Ghostfolio version of at least 3.91.0. Unknown, malformed, prerelease and older versions SHALL NOT certify native treatment. Do not require administrator credentials. Native-evidence enablement SHALL require image-backed version/profile read proof with a non-admin user and the minimum documented permissions; that runtime proof remains pending. Missing/denied split evidence SHALL mean unresolved representation, not an empty split list. Version read failure SHALL hold only work whose proof depends on native treatment; ordinary unaffected imports SHALL retain their existing requirements.

Version and ratio existence are necessary evidence, not acceptance of all event shapes. Require exact effective-quantity agreement and complete identity scope. Independently calculate effective quantities from complete activity/event evidence and compare with a holding response of matching account scope. A user-wide holding response SHALL NOT be treated as a target-account holding; reconcile its full visible account composition separately or keep the target proof unresolved. Cached holding agreement alone SHALL NOT certify a representation. Use bounded read-only observations on stale/unavailable holdings; never resend a financial request or flush caches to manufacture agreement. No epsilon, rounding or integer coercion SHALL turn a native quantity discrepancy into agreement. A failed native case SHALL remain unresolved. The initial increment SHALL send no native split request, activity repair, cash-component creation, identifier remapping or historical conversion.

Observed limits: the isolated frozen latest image on 2026-10-24 reported 3.92.0. Original forward-split cases reached exact quantities and zero after closing sales. The original four-lot reverse-split case returned 100.00000000000001 instead of 100 and did not reach its repeat/closing-sale checks. This does not certify every forward split or reject every reverse split. GET /api/v1/export with a restrictive activityIds filter returned meta.version in that lab. The retained user permissions include accessAdminControl; this is not non-admin runtime proof. Pinned profile GET source checks readMarketData or readMarketDataOfOwnAssetProfile rather than the separate administrator profile-list permission. Exact 3.91.0 source also contains that metadata; 3.91.0 runtime replay remains unobserved.

#### Scenario: Version and numerical evidence
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k native_evidence` mocks filtered version reads, stable boundary versions, prereleases, denied or missing profile reads, stale holdings, non-admin permission fixtures, exact native agreement and the observed reverse-split residue
- **THEN** only observed stable versions at or above 3.91.0 support native evidence, exact mismatch stays held, and no corporate-action financial request is dispatched.

### Requirement: Private persistent findings and notification state

CORPORATE_ACTION_STATE_FILE SHALL name a private regular keyed YAML file in a persistent mounted directory. It SHALL contain schema version, account/event bindings, retained evidence references and hashes, derived conclusions and held scope. Companion notification state SHALL retain delivery fingerprints separately from hold-critical evidence. It SHALL contain no credentials or financial approval control. Do not reuse the activity-review approval file or create a deduplication correspondence table. Original source evidence and freshly revalidated destination evidence SHALL remain authoritative. Retain complete validated report bytes in a private companion evidence directory under the persistent mount according to Automatic report rotation and safety evidence retention. Before any dependent BUY/SELL, durably bind each relevant report hash to its acquisition/source account, query, declared period, mapping fingerprint and schema. Record acquisition time durably and separately from the broker period, event dates and file modification time. Retain full event fields and report context needed to reconstruct the before/after chain, not only hashes or conclusions. Surviving evidence objects SHALL never be overwritten. Retirement SHALL follow the verified rotation contract; a retired object SHALL NOT remain an active required reference. Content hashes SHALL detect missing/changed objects; they are not signatures and do not authenticate a coordinated rewrite of both state and evidence. State and evidence are operator-controlled inputs, not approval controls.

A valid simulation with a configured writable path SHALL initialize a missing file after valid report acquisition, including section validation, and publish its bounded observed inventory. Invalid or failed source acquisition SHALL not initialize that account as covered. Simulation with unset path or unwritable/invalid state SHALL still perform otherwise safe reads and report the missing prerequisite, exit 1 and send the configured single Failure summary. It SHALL NOT claim readiness for live BUY/SELL. Simulation with valid state but an active hold SHALL also exit 1. A clean simulation with no hold SHALL retain exit 0, subject to existing failures. A live run with unset path, missing state, invalid schema, unsafe path, unsafe permissions or unavailable lock SHALL hold all BUY/SELL using that state and report the failure. It SHALL NOT create blank state and resume trading. Independent cash SHALL retain its existing guards. An unavailable path SHALL not prevent a safe identifier-free failure notification. Require atomic synced replacement, a cooperating-writer lock, private directory/file permissions and input-path collision checks. Existing state SHALL not be overwritten on failure or concurrent change. State loss requires simulation and review of the available history; it cannot establish coverage before the inspected windows. Preserve surviving event evidence and unresolved findings when rebuilding missing state. Rebuild them before any release decision. Missing evidence for a known unresolved event SHALL retain its hold. Deleting or reinitializing state SHALL not be offered as a release procedure. Loss of all local state/evidence cannot be distinguished reliably from a first installation; report that bootstrap limitation and require operator review of the bounded inventory, without claiming a trusted lifetime baseline.

Retain known unresolved events across shorter report windows. Reconcile current evidence instead of trusting editable conclusions. Publish the detailed report every run, including unchanged findings. If hold-critical evidence persistence fails, keep the affected BUY/SELL held. Failure or loss of only companion notification fingerprints SHALL repeat eligible notifications and log the delivery-state issue; it SHALL NOT invalidate independently valid financial evidence or create an additional trade hold. Report state and source limits explicitly; this feature does not establish a complete lifetime corporate-action inventory.

#### Scenario: Persistence and loss of evidence
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k persistence` exercises simulation initialization and missing/unwritable paths, live missing files, restarts, shorter windows, retained source objects, edited conclusions, surviving evidence after state loss, symlinks, permissions, locks, failed replacement, isolated notification-state loss and changed input hashes
- **THEN** durable findings remain private and inspectable, no stale conclusion releases a hold, invalid state cannot authorize BUY/SELL, and financial inputs remain unchanged.

### Requirement: Consolidated corporate-action reporting

Maintenance findings SHALL state whether the connector will retry automatically or whether the operator must restore evidence/state. Only the latter is a repeated required-action reminder.

With Apprise configured, active corporate-action holds SHALL notify once in each completed run. Nonblocking findings that require no user action SHALL notify on first appearance and material change. A nonblocking finding requiring user action SHALL instead join the required-action reminder on every completed run until resolved, including simulation. A previously held scope SHALL notify when fresh evidence permits resumption. Acknowledgment SHALL not suppress active holds. Simulation SHALL report these same observed conditions without claiming a financial execution. Coalesce these reasons with existing failure/expiry/forced-preview reasons into at most one dispatch per run. Unchanged nonblocking findings that require no user action SHALL remain in the detailed report without another notification. Companion delivery fingerprints SHALL not suppress required-action reminders. The one summary SHALL also include review, mapping, diagnostic and other required-action reasons under connector-runtime.

Notification bodies SHALL contain only safe fixed reason codes, counts and fixed plain-English instructions. No account, security, activity ID, amount, host or credential SHALL appear. Notification failure SHALL not change financial decisions or exit status. Record successful notification fingerprints only after confirmed delivery. Finalization SHALL reacquire the companion notification lock, revalidate its generation and atomically record only the exact findings that were sent. A concurrent change SHALL not be overwritten. Failure to record delivery SHALL leave the findings eligible next run. A send timeout SHALL remain unconfirmed even if it may later arrive; do not retry during that run. Failed delivery SHALL remain eligible next run. Notification fingerprints SHALL not release or create financial holds, consume financial approvals, or suppress active-hold notifications. A crash between send and persistence can duplicate a later notification; no exactly-once claim is made. Active holds and hold-critical state failures SHALL degrade the run; nonblocking historical discrepancies alone SHALL not.

#### Scenario: Repeated notifications and resumption
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k notifications` runs first, unchanged, changed, held, resolved, simulated, failed-delivery, timeout, post-send crash, concurrent finalization, unavailable companion state and Apprise-disabled cases alongside other run failures
- **THEN** one redacted summary follows the specified schedule, detailed findings appear every run, and notification delivery neither releases a hold nor changes the run outcome.

Each action-required corporate finding SHALL log at least WARNING with its reason, affected scope, configured state/evidence path when applicable and safe next step. Explain which proof is missing and which operations remain held. Do not present acknowledgment, state reset or journal edits as release. Private detail and public fixed-code/count redaction SHALL retain their separate roles. Existing trade holds, cash independence, exits and evidence retention SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Corporate and other required actions share delivery
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py tests/test_finalize.py -k operator_action_corporate` exercises unchanged action-required findings, unchanged non-action findings, review actions, delivery failure, fingerprint loss, resolution and simulation
- **THEN** required actions repeat each completed run in one redacted dispatch, non-action findings retain first/change/resumption behavior, and notification state never changes financial decisions or creates another dispatch.

### Requirement: Automatic report rotation and safety evidence retention

The connector SHALL retain ordinary acquired reports for a fixed 30 days, measured as 720 hours from durable UTC acquisition time. On the next normal run after expiration, it SHALL automatically retire reports that are not required safety evidence. This SHALL also apply in simulation: local evidence maintenance is allowed, financial writes remain forbidden. There SHALL be no new retention ENV setting, byte quota, object-count ceiling or separate maintenance command. A period without executions does not run background cleanup. Re-reading an existing acquisition or changing its file timestamps SHALL NOT renew its age. Invalid, missing or future acquisition timestamps SHALL NOT justify early removal. Existing evidence without trustworthy acquisition time SHALL receive a single durable migration observation time and the full 30-day grace period.

The connector SHALL retain a selective set of complete original XML reports, acquisition bindings and official date documents while needed for import safety. Preserve every known event and unresolved conflict, including unsupported shapes and revised or cancelled evidence. Preserve the report context, trade chain, independent opening quantities, identity associations and continuous declared coverage needed to reconstruct and revalidate those events. A closed position, matching holding, prior reconciliation, manual acknowledgment or shorter current window SHALL NOT alone make evidence redundant. Retained evidence SHALL remain reparsable and hash-verifiable. Derived summaries or an observed current decision alone SHALL NOT replace required originals.

Automatic maintenance SHALL remove redundant acquisitions from the safety set only after proving that the surviving source evidence preserves these facts and the necessary reconstruction. Identical facts in overlapping reports SHALL NOT cause permanent retention of every daily copy. Archive expiry SHALL NOT manufacture new coverage, erase a known event/conflict or release a hold. When safe retirement cannot be proved, preserve the uncertain evidence and report the limitation. Ordinary archive size and retained safety work SHALL not grow with repeated acquisitions alone after the retention window. Safety storage MAY grow with distinct useful events, changed evidence and their required chains; no fixed total disk bound is claimed.

Maintenance SHALL use the existing writer lock, safe paths and durable atomic publication. Publish and verify surviving references before removing eligible files. A restart at any stage SHALL preserve all required originals and recover a complete committed reference set. Cleanup retries SHALL be idempotent. State recovery SHALL distinguish intentional retirement from lost required evidence; it SHALL NOT restore retired references merely by finding an obsolete binding. Automatically remove only connector-owned evidence that is not a configured input or an external alias. Rotation SHALL NOT affect mappings, official input documents, Ghostfolio data, activity-review proposals or correction journals. In particular, pending or confirmed financial journals SHALL not expire through this policy.

Failure of maintenance alone SHALL log a clear warning and enter the existing consolidated notification when configured. It SHALL NOT change exit status or suspend BUY/SELL if all required current and historical evidence remains valid and durable. It SHALL retry on the next run. Failure to acquire, validate, persist or recover required safety evidence SHALL retain the existing affected BUY/SELL hold and failure result. Independent cash and unrelated destinations SHALL retain their existing guards. No failed cleanup SHALL trigger a financial resend or a state reset.

Reconciliation SHALL retain its existing diagnostic logs and proposals. The user handles manual cleanup and reruns analysis. There SHALL be no new persistent reconciliation job, inactivity timeout, automatic archive replay or automatic protection of a user investigation. README SHALL link directly to the corporate-action guide. That guide SHALL explain the fixed retention period, safety exceptions, local maintenance in simulation, failure behavior and the need to keep a private copy or obtain another broker export for longer investigations. It SHALL NOT claim lifetime history or recovery of expired archives. New logs and notifications SHALL preserve existing redaction rules.

Baseline: candidate README states full report retention grows with acquisitions; the retained-evidence tests verify originals and recovery but implement no expiry. Activity review builds current source events and keeps its own proposal and correction journal. Commands below specify future offline acceptance, not current passing evidence.
