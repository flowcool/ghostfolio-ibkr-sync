## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Consolidated corporate-action reporting

Maintenance findings SHALL state whether the connector will retry automatically or whether the operator must restore evidence/state. Only the latter is a repeated required-action reminder.

With Apprise configured, active corporate-action holds SHALL notify once in each completed run. Nonblocking findings that require no user action SHALL notify on first appearance and material change. A nonblocking finding requiring user action SHALL instead join the required-action reminder on every completed run until resolved, including simulation. A previously held scope SHALL notify when fresh evidence permits resumption. Acknowledgment SHALL not suppress active holds. Simulation SHALL report these same observed conditions without claiming a financial execution. Coalesce these reasons with existing failure/expiry/forced-preview reasons into at most one dispatch per run. Unchanged nonblocking findings that require no user action SHALL remain in the detailed report without another notification. Companion delivery fingerprints SHALL not suppress required-action reminders. The one summary SHALL also include review, mapping, diagnostic and other required-action reasons under connector-runtime.

Notification bodies SHALL contain only safe fixed reason codes, counts and fixed plain-English instructions. No account, security, activity ID, amount, host or credential SHALL appear. Notification failure SHALL not change financial decisions or exit status. Record successful notification fingerprints only after confirmed delivery. Finalization SHALL reacquire the companion notification lock, revalidate its generation and atomically record only the exact findings that were sent. A concurrent change SHALL not be overwritten. Failure to record delivery SHALL leave the findings eligible next run. A send timeout SHALL remain unconfirmed even if it may later arrive; do not retry during that run. Failed delivery SHALL remain eligible next run. Notification fingerprints SHALL not release or create financial holds, consume financial approvals, or suppress active-hold notifications. A crash between send and persistence can duplicate a later notification; no exactly-once claim is made. Active holds and hold-critical state failures SHALL degrade the run; nonblocking historical discrepancies alone SHALL not.

#### Scenario: Repeated notifications and resumption
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py -k notifications` runs first, unchanged, changed, held, resolved, simulated, failed-delivery, timeout, post-send crash, concurrent finalization, unavailable companion state and Apprise-disabled cases alongside other run failures
- **THEN** one redacted summary follows the specified schedule, detailed findings appear every run, and notification delivery neither releases a hold nor changes the run outcome.

Each action-required corporate finding SHALL log at least WARNING with its reason, affected scope, configured state/evidence path when applicable and safe next step. Explain which proof is missing and which operations remain held. Do not present acknowledgment, state reset or journal edits as release. Private detail and public fixed-code/count redaction SHALL retain their separate roles. Existing trade holds, cash independence, exits and evidence retention SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Corporate and other required actions share delivery
- **WHEN** `python -m pytest -q tests/test_corporate_actions.py tests/test_finalize.py -k operator_action_corporate` exercises unchanged action-required findings, unchanged non-action findings, review actions, delivery failure, fingerprint loss, resolution and simulation
- **THEN** required actions repeat each completed run in one redacted dispatch, non-action findings retain first/change/resumption behavior, and notification state never changes financial decisions or creates another dispatch.
